OUR DEPARTMENTS

CARDIOLOGY

The Cardiology Department provides complete diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of heart diseases. Our experienced specialists use advanced technology to treat heart attacks, hypertension, heart failure, arrhythmia, and coronary artery disease while ensuring compassionate patient care.

NEUROLOGY

Our Neurology Department specializes in diagnosing and treating disorders of the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system, including stroke, epilepsy, migraine, Parkinson's disease, and nerve disorders.

ORTHOPEDICS

The Orthopedics Department treats fractures, arthritis, sports injuries, spinal disorders, and joint replacement with modern surgical and rehabilitation facilities.

PEDIATRICS

We provide complete healthcare for infants, children, and teenagers, including vaccinations, growth monitoring, nutrition guidance, and treatment for childhood illnesses.
